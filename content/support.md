## Contact {#people}

If you find any bugs or you have any ideas on developing ngIRCd, it is best to
put them into the [bug tracker](#bugs) of ngIRCd. There is also a
[mailing list](#ml) for discussions and questions concerning ngIRCd (and you
don't need an account for posting).

Alexander Barton (<alex@barton.de>) had the idea to develop ngIRCd; he is
responsible for big parts of today's source code.

BTW: We are always happy to receive patches, feedback and additional
supporters!

ngIRCd in IRC: <irc://irc.barton.de/ngircd>.

## Bug tracker {#bugs}

We are using the bug tracking system at [GitHub](https://github.com/) to avoid
losing sight of existing bugs and suggestions for features in ngIRCd.

You can get [information](https://github.com/ngircd/ngircd/issues) on all
known bugs (and probable fixes) there, and you can create new issues for new
bugs or wishlist items, too.

Please try to describe your problem as precisely as possible, and of course
patches (pull requests) are always very welcome!

## Mailing list {#ml}

There is an (English) mailing list, which covers all technical questions
around ngIRCd: compilation, installation, configuration and further
development and upgrading. Furthermore, it is used to announce updates and
innovations.

You can sign up via the
[homepage of the mailing list](https://lists.barton.de/mailman3/postorius/lists/ngircd.lists.barton.de),
but this is not required for posting to the list.

All posts are archived in the
[list archive](https://lists.barton.de/mailman3/hyperkitty/list/ngircd@lists.barton.de/).

Email address: <ngircd@lists.barton.de>.
